Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Time Sheet Pro ("Time Sheet Pro", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

Time Sheet Pro is designed to help users record work shifts, calculate working hours, generate PDF timesheet reports, and share reports through applications such as WhatsApp and email.

1. Information You Enter Into the App

Time Sheet Pro allows you to enter information necessary to create and manage your timesheets.

This may include:

  • Employee or user name
  • Work date
  • Shift start time
  • Shift end time
  • Break or other time information entered by you
  • Daily working hours
  • Weekly working hours
  • Two-week working-hour totals
  • Notes or other information you voluntarily enter

The information you enter is used primarily to calculate your working hours and generate timesheet reports.

We do not require you to provide your name or other personal information unless you choose to enter it into the App.

2. Local Storage

Time Sheet Pro is designed to store your timesheet information locally on your device.

Your manually entered timesheet information is not automatically uploaded to our servers merely because you use the App.

The App may use local device storage to save your timesheet information and application settings so that your information remains available when you reopen the App.

If you delete the App or clear its application data, locally stored information may be permanently deleted.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate security and backups for information stored on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information entered into Time Sheet Pro may be used to:

  • Record your work shifts.
  • Calculate daily working hours.
  • Calculate weekly working hours.
  • Calculate two-week working hours.
  • Organize your timesheet information.
  • Generate PDF reports.
  • Prepare reports for sharing.
  • Provide the features and functionality of the App.
  • Improve application performance.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Display advertisements.

We do not use your timesheet information to create advertising profiles.

4. PDF Reports

Time Sheet Pro allows you to generate PDF timesheet reports.

A generated PDF may contain information that you entered into the App, including:

  • Your name, if entered
  • Dates
  • Shift start and end times
  • Working-hour calculations
  • Weekly totals
  • Two-week totals
  • Notes, if entered

PDF files are generated on your device for the purpose of providing the reporting functionality of the App.

The PDF is not automatically sent to us.

5. Sharing Through WhatsApp, Email, and Other Applications

Time Sheet Pro may allow you to share a generated PDF report through applications installed on your device.

This may include:

  • WhatsApp
  • Email applications
  • Messaging applications
  • Cloud storage applications
  • Other compatible sharing applications

When you select a sharing option, the PDF is transferred using the operating system's sharing functionality or the selected third-party application.

For example, if you select WhatsApp, the PDF is provided to WhatsApp for transmission according to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

Time Sheet Pro does not control how WhatsApp, email providers, or other third-party applications process information after you choose to share a file with them.

6. Advertising

Time Sheet Pro is supported by advertising and may display advertisements from third-party advertising providers.

We may use Google AdMob or another third-party advertising provider to display advertisements in the App.

Advertising providers may automatically collect certain technical information from your device, which may include:

  • IP address
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Advertising identifier
  • App information
  • General location information derived from IP address
  • Information about interactions with advertisements
  • Other information necessary to provide and measure advertisements

Depending on applicable laws, your device settings, and consent choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Important: Your timesheet records and manually entered work information are not provided to advertisers by Time Sheet Pro for the purpose of advertising.

7. Advertising Preferences and Consent

Where required by applicable law, Time Sheet Pro may request your consent before displaying personalized advertisements or allowing certain advertising-related data processing.

You may also have options provided by your device, operating system, or advertising provider to control advertising preferences.

8. Analytics and Crash Reporting

To maintain and improve the App, we may use technical services that provide analytics or crash reporting.

If services such as Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics are integrated into the App, these services may collect technical information such as:

  • Device model
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • Crash information
  • Diagnostic information
  • Performance information
  • General application usage information

These services are used to identify problems, improve stability, and improve the overall performance of Time Sheet Pro.

9. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Time Sheet Pro does not require the following information for its core time-tracking functionality:

  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Call history
  • Text messages
  • Photos or videos
  • Passwords
  • Financial account information

The App does not need access to your contacts in order to share a PDF through WhatsApp or email.

10. Location Information

Time Sheet Pro does not intentionally collect or track your precise GPS location.

The App's time-tracking functionality does not require location tracking.

However, third-party advertising or analytics services may independently process approximate location information derived from technical information such as an IP address, subject to their own policies and applicable laws.

11. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your manually entered timesheet information.

Your information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to provide specific functionality or when you intentionally interact with those services.

These situations may include:

  • Displaying advertisements
  • Analytics or crash reporting
  • Sharing a PDF through WhatsApp
  • Sending a PDF through email
  • Using the operating system's sharing functionality

12. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information handled by the App.

Because your primary timesheet information may be stored locally on your device, the security of that information also depends on the security of your device.

We recommend protecting your device with a password, PIN, passcode, biometric authentication, or another available security mechanism.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Data Retention

Because timesheet information may be stored locally on your device, it generally remains there until you:

  • Delete the information through the App;
  • Clear the App's data;
  • Uninstall the App; or
  • Otherwise remove the stored information from your device.

The exact behavior may depend on your device and operating system.

Information processed by third-party services, such as advertising or analytics providers, may be retained according to their respective policies.

14. Deleting Your Information

You may delete your timesheet information using the deletion features available within Time Sheet Pro.

You may also remove locally stored application data through your device settings where supported.

Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored information, depending on your operating system and device configuration.

Because Time Sheet Pro does not require a user account for its core functionality, there may be no centralized account database containing your timesheet records.

15. Children's Privacy

Time Sheet Pro is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected by us, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.
